1. Does the law apply to you?

You are covered if you use an automated employment decision tool (AEDT) — a machine-learning-based screener, ranker, or scorer — for hiring or promotion decisions involving positions based in New York City, including remote roles open to NYC residents.

Common tools that qualify: AI resume screeners, interview-ranking systems, and skills-assessment scorers that substantially assist or replace discretionary decisions. If your ATS vendor calls it "AI-powered," assume it qualifies until a compliance review says otherwise.

2. The three obligations (overview)

  • Annual bias audit — independent audit within the last 12 months
  • Public summary — posted on your careers site
  • Candidate notice — 10 business days before AEDT use
