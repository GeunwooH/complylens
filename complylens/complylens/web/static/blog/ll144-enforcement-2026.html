NYC LL144 Enforcement in 2026: What the Comptroller's Audit Means for Employers

The New York State Comptroller's December 2025 audit (2024-N-6) concluded that DCWP's enforcement of Local Law 144 has been ineffective. In reviewing the same 32 companies DCWP examined, auditors identified at least 17 instances of potential non-compliance — where DCWP found just one. During two years of enforcement, only two AEDT complaints reached the agency.

The audit's recommendations push DCWP toward proactive investigation, and law firms are advising clients to expect tighter enforcement through 2026. Penalties under LL144 accumulate daily: $500 for a first violation and $500–$1,500 per day after, per tool.

The practical takeaway: the compliance clock has been running since July 2023, and the enforcement posture is now tightening. An annual bias audit with a posted public summary is the single requirement that keeps employers out of this exposure.
