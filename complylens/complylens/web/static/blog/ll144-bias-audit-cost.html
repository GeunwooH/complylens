What drives the price

  • Independence requirement: the audit must be performed by someone with no financial interest in the tool — this is the main cost driver in the full-audit tier.
  • Data complexity: clean CSV exports cost less than messy multi-ATS integrations.
  • Turnaround: automated analysis is 72 hours; full audits with sign-off take longer.
  • Multi-jurisdiction: adding Illinois/Colorado/EU obligations raises the price.

The cost of NOT doing it

Penalties run $500 first violation, $500–$1,500 per day after, per tool — and enforcement went proactive in January 2026. Six months of non-compliance on one tool is a $67,500–$270,000 exposure. The audit is the cheapest insurance you can buy in this area.
