EU AI Act Compliance Checklist for SaaS Providers

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) applies to any provider placing AI systems on the EU market — including SaaS platforms with EU users. With transparency obligations applying from 2 August 2026, this checklist is the minimum starting point.

1. Classify your system

  • Confirmed whether your product is an AI system (Art. 3(1)) — ML or logic-based systems generating outputs.
  • Checked prohibited practices (Art. 5): manipulation, social scoring, real-time biometric ID, emotion inference in workplace/education.
  • Determined high-risk status (Annex III): employment, credit, education, biometrics, critical infrastructure, and more.
  • Checked GPAI status (Art. 51): general-purpose models above 10^25 FLOPs.

2. Risk management (Art. 9)

  • Risk management system established: identification, evaluation, mitigation of risks to health, safety, fundamental rights.
  • Reasonably foreseeable misuse documented.
  • Residual risk accepted at an appropriate level, with evidence.

3. Documentation

  • Technical documentation prepared per Art. 11 / Annex IV (architecture, data, oversight, accuracy/robustness results).
  • Logging capabilities (Art. 12) for high-risk systems.
  • Record of changes and version history.

4. Transparency (Art. 50)

  • Users informed they are interacting with an AI system (chatbots/conversational AI).
  • Synthetic content labelled (deepfakes, AI-generated media).
  • Emotion recognition / biometric categorisation disclosed where applicable.

5. Incident readiness (Art. 72–73)

  • Incident reporting process defined (serious incidents → authority within 15 days).
  • Monitoring and post-market surveillance plan (Art. 72).
  • Complaint channel established.

6. Governance

  • Responsible person designated for the compliance file.
  • European Union representative appointed (non-EU providers, Art. 22).
  • Conformity assessment route identified for high-risk systems.
