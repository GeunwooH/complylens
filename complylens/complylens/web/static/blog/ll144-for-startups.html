Why startups get caught off guard

  • LL144 applies to any size company — there's no small-employer exemption.
  • Remote roles open to NYC residents count, even if you have no NYC office.
  • Your ATS vendor's "AI-powered" feature may already qualify as an AEDT.
  • Penalties are per-day ($500–$1,500/day/tool) — a year of non-compliance is a six-figure exposure even for a small company.
