With enforcement becoming proactive in January 2026 and the first fines issued, demand for LL144 compliance work is real — and so is the range of providers. Here is what to check before you buy.

The five checks

CheckWhy it mattersRed flags
1. MethodologyThe audit must follow DCWP Final Rules: selection rates, impact ratios, four-fifths assessment (and score-based metrics for scoring tools).No mention of impact ratios or four-fifths; "we use AI to check fairness" without a defined statistical method.
2. IndependenceThe auditor must have no financial interest in the tool, its vendor, or your company.The provider also sells the tool being audited, or consulting to the same client.
3. TurnaroundYour audit must be current (within 12 months) and you need it before any NYC hiring campaign.Weeks-long queues; no committed timeline.
4. DeliverablesDetailed report + public summary (post on careers site) + candidate notice (10 business days).Only a score or a one-page letter; no summary/notice templates.
5. Price transparencyFlat pricing exists in the market ($299 automated toolkits to $2,500+ full audits).Quote-only with no range; surprise add-ons.

What the market looks like (2026)

  • Full independent audits: $1,500–$7,500+ — includes auditor sign-off, legal review; required for full legal effectiveness.
