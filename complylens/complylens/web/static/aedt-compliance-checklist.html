AEDT Compliance Checklist — NYC Local Law 144

Use this checklist to verify compliance for every automated employment decision tool (AEDT) you use for NYC-based positions. Enforcement became proactive in January 2026 and the first fines have been issued — this is no longer a paper-only law.

Coverage

  • Inventory every AI-assisted tool used in hiring or promotion (screeners, rankers, scorers, chatbots).
  • Confirmed which tools qualify as AEDTs (ML/statistical, substantially assist or replace discretionary decisions).
  • Identified which positions are NYC-based, including remote roles open to NYC residents.

Bias audit

  • Current independent bias audit completed within the last 12 months (selection rates, impact ratios, four-fifths assessment per DCWP Final Rules).
  • Auditor is independent — no financial interest in the tool, vendor, or employer.
  • Score-based tools assessed with median-threshold scoring rates.
  • Re-audit calendar set for 12 months from the audit date.

Public summary

  • Summary of the most recent bias audit posted publicly on the careers site.
  • Summary includes tool identification and per-category selection rates / impact ratios.
  • Summary URL is linked from job postings that use the AEDT.

Candidate notice

  • Notice provided at least 10 business days before AEDT use.
  • Notice identifies the tool and the job qualifications/characteristics it evaluates.
  • Alternative selection process offered and contact provided.

Penalty awareness

  • Understood exposure: $500 first violation, $500–$1,500/day after, per tool (180 days × 1 tool ≈ $67,500–$270,000).
  • Assigned a compliance owner and documented decisions.
