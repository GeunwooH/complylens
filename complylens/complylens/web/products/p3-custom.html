Checklist by jurisdiction

NYC — LL144

[ ] Bias audit completed within 12 months (independent auditor)
[ ] Public summary posted on careers site
[ ] Candidate notice 10 business days before use
[ ] Alternative process offered

Illinois — HB 3773

[ ] Notice to Illinois applicants that AI is used and which characteristics are evaluated
[ ] No discrimination via AI-assisted decisions
[ ] Documentation of tool characteristics and role in decisions

Colorado — AI Act

[ ] High-risk classification determined (consequential decisions = employment)
[ ] Impact assessment conducted and retained
[ ] Notice + right to review data + appeal process
[ ] Human review capability documented

California — ADMT (preparation)

[ ] Track rulemaking status (2027 scheduled)
[ ] Prepare risk-assessment process aligned with CCPA framework
[ ] Notice/opt-out workflow designed
