How to evaluate a vendor (scorecard)

[ ] 1. Methodology matches DCWP Final Rules (selection rate, impact ratio, four-fifths)
[ ] 2. Independence disclosed (no interest in tool/vendor/employer)
[ ] 3. Committed turnaround (72h–2 weeks)
[ ] 4. Deliverables: detailed report + public summary + candidate notice
[ ] 5. Price transparency (flat or clear range)
[ ] 6. Data handling: US processing, no AI training, deletion policy
[ ] 7. References or sample report available

Total /14 — 11+ = strong candidate; 7–10 = verify gaps; <7 = keep looking

4. Required deliverables:
   a. Selection rates and impact ratios by protected category and intersections

   d. Public summary suitable for posting
   e. Candidate notice template (10 business days)
5. Independence: confirm no financial interest in the tool, its vendor, or us
6. Turnaround: [date] deadline

8. Data handling: processing location, retention, deletion, no AI training

Submit to: [email] by [date]. Include methodology description and sample report.

Buying recommendation

For most employers: run the automated statistical analysis first ($299–$1,500) to see your numbers, then decide whether independent sign-off is needed for legal effectiveness. The four-fifths result is identical either way — the sign-off is the legal wrapper.
