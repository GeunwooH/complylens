SOC 2 Under $5k: Startup Evidence Binder v1

This playbook is for a bootstrapped SaaS team with fewer than ten people that needs a credible SOC 2 readiness path without buying an enterprise GRC platform. It focuses on SOC 2 Type I preparation: proving that your controls are designed and ready at a point in time.

Important boundary: SOC 2 is an attestation performed by an independent CPA firm. This information product is not an attestation, certification, legal advice, audit, or guarantee that an auditor will issue a report. Confirm scope and criteria with your selected CPA firm.

3. Minimum control set for a small SaaS

  • Access: SSO or MFA, least privilege, joiner/mover/leaver process, quarterly review.
  • Change management: pull-request review, deployment approval, rollback path, production log.
  • Incident response: severity levels, contacts, customer notice decision, tabletop record.
  • Availability: backup schedule, restore test, monitoring, recovery objectives.
  • Vendor risk: subprocessors, security review, contract owner, annual reassessment.
  • Risk management: named risks, likelihood, impact, treatment, residual risk and acceptance.

6. Questions for an auditor

  1. Which Trust Services Criteria and system boundary are included in the proposal?
  2. Is this a Type I or Type II engagement, and what period or point-in-time date applies?
  3. Which evidence samples and control owners will you expect?
  4. What is excluded: subservice organizations, availability, privacy, or customer responsibilities?
  5. Which remediation work is advisory and which work must be completed before testing?

Definition of ready

You are ready to request a scoped proposal when the system boundary is written, owners are named, the minimum control set has evidence locations, open gaps have dates, and management can explain the remaining risk. Do not call the company “SOC 2 compliant” before an independent report supports that statement.
