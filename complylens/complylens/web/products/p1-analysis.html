LL144 Readiness Assessment Toolkit

Product: ComplyLens LL144 Readiness Assessment Toolkit — a structured self-assessment that determines whether your company is covered by NYC Local Law 144 and what you must do, plus the templates you need to act. Informational analysis — not a legally effective bias audit.

Step 1 — Coverage determination

COVERAGE CHECK — complete for each tool used in hiring/promotion

1. Is the tool an AEDT?
   [ ] Uses machine learning, statistical modelling, or data analytics
   [ ] Substantially assists or replaces discretionary decision-making
   [ ] Screens, evaluates, or ranks candidates/employees
   If all three: it is an AEDT under DCWP Final Rules.

2. Does it touch NYC positions?
   [ ] Positions based in New York City
   [ ] Remote roles open to NYC residents
   If either: LL144 applies regardless of where your company is headquartered.

3. Last audit date:
   [ ] Bias audit completed within the last 12 months?
   [ ] Public summary posted on careers site?
   [ ] Candidate notice provided 10 business days before use?
   If ANY answer is NO for a covered tool: you are non-compliant today.

Step 2 — Compliance gap & exposure estimate

GAP & EXPOSURE WORKSHEET

Per DCWP penalty schedule (§ 6-81 RCNY):
- First violation: $375 (default $500)
- Second violation: $1,350 (default $1,500)
- Third+ violation: $1,500
- Each day of non-compliant use = a separate violation.

Estimated exposure = days_non_compliant × per_day_rate × number_of_tools
Example: 180 days × $1,500 × 2 tools = $540,000 upper-bound exposure
         180 days × $375 × 2 tools = $135,000 lower-bound exposure

Realistic midpoint for planning: $135K–$540K range. Compare against the cost
of a current audit (typically $1,500–$2,500 for automated analysis).

Step 3 — Action plan templates

A. AUDIT RFP BRIEF (use to solicit independent auditor quotes)
- Tool name + version + vendor
- Data available: candidate records with protected categories (sex, ethnicity)
- Selection outcome or score field
- NYC position volume and locations
- Required deliverables: selection rates, impact ratios, four-fifths assessment,
  public summary, candidate notice
- Timeline required (e.g. 72 hours for automated analysis)

B. PUBLIC SUMMARY TEMPLATE (post on careers site)
  [Tool name] is used to screen candidates for NYC-based positions.
  A bias audit of this tool was completed on [date] by [auditor].
  Selection rates by protected category: [table]
  Impact ratios: [table]
  Contact for alternative process: [email]

C. CANDIDATE NOTICE TEMPLATE (10 business days before use)
  This position uses [tool name], an automated employment decision tool,
  to screen candidates. You may request an alternative selection process
  by contacting [email]. [Date]
